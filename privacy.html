Privacy Policy — Spooky Extravaganza Publisher

Effective October 10, 2026

What the app is

Spooky Extravaganza Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Spooky Extravaganza Pinterest account to create and schedule that account's own Pins through the Pinterest API.

Data we access

  • The account owner's own boards (to choose where a Pin is saved) and own Pins (to confirm a Pin was created).
  • We do not access, collect or store data about any other Pinterest user.

Data we store

  • The OAuth access token for the owner's account, stored only on the owner's computer in a file readable by the owner alone. It is never shared, sold, or sent anywhere except to Pinterest's API.
  • The images and text of our own Pins, which we create ourselves.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. No analytics, advertising or tracking is built into the app.

Retention and deletion

The access token can be revoked at any time from Pinterest's settings, and the local token file is deleted when the app is no longer used.

Affiliate links

Our Pins may contain Amazon affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Clicking those links is governed by Amazon's own privacy notice.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/igalratiner/spooky-extravaganza/issues.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
